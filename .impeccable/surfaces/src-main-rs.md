Scope: whole terminal app (menu room, studio lobby, minigames, results, spectator). Mode: experience.

## Direction contract

THESIS: PartyLine is a live TV game show you watch from your couch and then get pulled into. It refuses the green-on-black hacker terminal and the neon arcade cabinet.

OWN-WORLD: Night living room in half-block pixel art: indigo dark, amber lamp, CRT glow spilling on walls tinted by what the screen shows. Inside the TV, a studio: royal stage blue ground, marquee bulb gold borders that chase, podium red, flap-white counters that flip. Each player owns one saturated hue stripe everywhere.

STORY: Friends see the room, steer the TV menu, dive through the screen into the show, compete in minigames, crown a champion on the podium. Spectators watch the broadcast and pick a camera.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Room fills the terminal. CRT TV centered, about 60% width. Screen shows the PARTYLINE channel logo in big text and the menu (HOST, JOIN, NAME, QUIT) with a gold selection bar. Lamp right, couch silhouette across the bottom. Enter: camera dollies into the screen (700 ms, strong ease-in-out), CRT power-on line reveals the studio.

FORM: game-show studio framed by the user-pinned TV-in-a-room. Seed b6dcc99f. Assigned split-flap board presented; user asked for a recommendation; my top pick taken with the user's TV pin.
Raise (split-flap): every score and code change flips through digits with mechanical cadence.
Raise (Raygun desk): calm screens hold a strict grid; GO, KO and WIN break it with giant type and shake.
Raise (accretion disk): elimination has one state everywhere: player hue drains to grey and the row collapses.
Raise (event display): spectator focus isolates one player and dims the rest.
